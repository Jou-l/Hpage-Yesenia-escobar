    Afro-Colombian writer, scholar and advocate

    Afro-Colombian writer, poet, and scholar from Barranquilla. My work focuses on Afro-Latin American literature and culture, particularly the contributions of Afro-descendant women writers. Drawing on African philosophies, Black studies, affect theory, and decolonial thought, I explore the intersections of ancestral memory, spirituality, language, and cultural production.
